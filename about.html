Who We Are

A dealer, supplier & manufacturer for second-life lithium.

Lithium WorkX specializes in refurbished and second-life lithium cells. We repurpose end-of-life lithium batteries by extracting usable cells, testing and grading them as A-grade working inventory, and responsibly recycling the rest. Our repurposed cells go into building new EV batteries and energy storage solutions — closing the loop on lithium waste.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To make reliable, affordable lithium power accessible to every builder in India by recovering the enormous value locked inside end-of-life battery packs.

  • Test and grade every cell to an honest A-grade standard
  • Supply consistent, batch-matched inventory for B2B bulk orders
  • Divert lithium waste from landfill through responsible recycling

Our Vision

A circular lithium economy where no usable cell is wasted — powering the next generation of EVs and energy storage from resources already in circulation.

  • Become India's most trusted name in second-life lithium
  • Scale domestic EV battery manufacturing from repurposed cells
  • Set the benchmark for transparency in cell grading
