About the company

A B2B partner for circular battery technology

Lithiumworkx is a B2B battery technology and circular energy solutions company focused on helping businesses recover, evaluate, rebuild and redeploy battery assets for practical energy applications.

We work with battery manufacturers, EV companies, solar businesses, battery assemblers and industrial organizations to develop refurbished battery packs, second-life energy systems, custom industrial battery solutions and BESS applications.

Through testing, grading, refurbishment and repurposing, Lithiumworkx aims to extend battery life, recover usable energy capacity and build a more efficient circular battery ecosystem for businesses.

What drives us

Our mission and vision

Our Mission

To help businesses across the EV, solar and industrial sectors recover maximum value from every battery — through rigorous testing, grading and refurbishment that extends usable life and reduces waste.

Our Vision

To build a dependable, pan-India circular battery ecosystem where every recovered cell finds a practical second life — powering industries more sustainably and affordably.

How it works

Our system

Every battery that enters our ecosystem moves through the same structured system — designed to recover the maximum practical value from each asset.

01

Recover

We source and recover battery assets from manufacturers, EV fleets and industrial partners.

02

Each battery undergoes structured testing and grading to assess real-world performance.

03

Rebuild

Suitable cells are refurbished into dependable, application-ready battery packs.

04

Redeploy

Rebuilt systems return to service across EV, solar, backup and industrial applications.

Inside the workshop

Our process

At Lithiumworkx, every battery goes through a structured process of Source, Inspect, Test, Grade, Refurbish/Repurpose and Deploy. We begin by identifying suitable battery assets, followed by physical inspection and technical screening. Each battery is then tested for key performance parameters such as capacity, voltage and internal resistance before being graded according to its actual condition and application suitability. Suitable batteries are refurbished into practical battery systems or repurposed for second-life energy applications, allowing recovered battery capacity to return to useful B2B applications.
