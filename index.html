Who We Are

Powering the circular lithium battery economy.

Lithium WorkX is a dealer, supplier, and manufacturer specializing in refurbished and second-life lithium cells. We repurpose end-of-life lithium batteries by extracting usable cells, testing and grading them as A-grade working inventory, and responsibly recycling the rest. Our repurposed cells go into building new EV batteries and energy storage solutions — closing the loop on lithium waste.

Our Mission

Giving lithium batteries a second life to power a sustainable future.

Refurbished lithium cells are extracted from end-of-life battery packs, individually tested for capacity and charge retention, and graded. Cells that pass our quality checks are sold as A-grade working inventory. Those that don't meet standards are responsibly recycled.
We operate on a B2B model with price on quote. Simply reach out via DM, email, or phone with your requirements — cell type, quantity, and specifications. We'll share pricing and availability from our current warehouse stock.
Our customers include e-bike builders, power tool repair shops, EV and ESS companies, solar energy integrators, and RC hobbyists. We serve businesses across India looking for reliable, tested lithium cells at competitive prices.

Our Process

How we give lithium batteries a second life.

From collection to grading to repurposing — a transparent 3-step loop that turns waste into reliable, tested power for your builds.

01
Collect

Source end-of-life lithium battery packs from across India.

02
Test & Grade

Extract cells, test each one, grade usable cells as A-grade, and scrap the rest.

03
Repurpose

Build new EV batteries or supply bulk cells to businesses Pan India.
