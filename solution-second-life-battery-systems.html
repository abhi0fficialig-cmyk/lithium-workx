Second-Life Battery Systems

Our Second-Life Battery Systems give retired EV and industrial lithium cells a productive second run — repurposed into custom energy storage systems for backup, solar, and light industrial use. Retired cells that still hold 70–85% of their original capacity are graded, matched and rebuilt into application-specific systems that are safer, cheaper and better for the planet than sending cells to recycling.
