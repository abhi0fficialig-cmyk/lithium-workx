Product Overview

The BAK 21700 5000mAh is a second-life 21700 lithium cell recovered from end-of-life battery packs, then individually tested, capacity-matched and graded by the Lithium WorkX team. Only cells that pass our full electrical and physical inspection are classed as A-grade working inventory — the rest are routed into responsible recycling.

Every batch we ship is voltage-sorted and internal-resistance matched so pack builders get consistent, predictable performance during assembly. Cells are supplied clean, at storage charge, and packed for safe transit anywhere in India.

How We Test & Grade

  • Full capacity discharge test against rated mAh, with low-capacity cells rejected.
  • Internal resistance (mΩ) measurement and batch matching for pack consistency.
  • Open-circuit voltage and self-discharge monitoring over a rest period.
  • Visual and mechanical check — no dents, leaks, corrosion or wrapper damage.
  • Final binning into matched groups for predictable pack assembly.

Typical Applications

  • Custom EV battery manufacturing
  • Long-range 2W & 3W packs
  • Residential ESS
  • Solar hybrid inverters

Handling, Warranty & Support

These are second-life cells intended for professional pack builders and integrators. We recommend assembly with a suitable BMS, correct fusing and balanced charging. Each bulk order is backed by a replacement guarantee on any cell falling outside the stated A-grade tolerance on arrival, and our team shares the current tested stock list on request.
